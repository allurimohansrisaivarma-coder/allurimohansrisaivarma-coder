<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/%F0%9F%A5%87%201st%20Place-IEEE%20Campus%20Ops-b8860b?style=flat-square" alt="1st place, IEEE Campus Ops"/>&nbsp;<sub><a href="https://github.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/PA-TA_intranet">Repository →</a></sub>

Ask *“Is it safe to go to sea today?”* out loud, in Malayalam or English, and get a clear **SAFE / CAUTION / UNSAFE** answer for your boat and your harbour. Then Sea Safe watches the trip and raises the alarm if you don't come home. Piloted for Vizhinjam, Kerala.

- **Rules decide, AI explains:** a tested risk engine sets the verdict; the voice assistant only puts it into words
- **Trip watch with automatic SOS** to family, buddy boats and the Coast Guard, plus SMS SOS that works without internet
- **Drift search zone** from current and wind, showing where a missing boat is likely to be at +1, +3 and +6 hours
- **Harbour-mouth check** for breaking waves, and community hazard reports that count only once another fisher confirms them

### FalconAero &nbsp;·&nbsp; Airport disruption recovery
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/%F0%9F%8F%85%204th%20Overall%20in%20the%20UAE-UAE%20Hackathon%202026%20%C2%B7%20Pioneers-0e7490?style=flat-square" alt="4th overall in the UAE, UAE Hackathon 2026, Pioneers track"/>&nbsp;<sub>GCAA challenge · Team Falconix · <a href="https://github.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/FalconAero-UAEhack">Repository →</a></sub>

<a href="https://github.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/FalconAero-UAEhack"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/FalconAero-UAEhack/main/docs/screenshots/01-operations-airspace.png" width="100%" alt="FalconAero operations view: flight strips, 40 NM airspace view, stand timeline and alerts"/></a>

An event-driven decision-support engine for an Airport Operations Centre. It ingests schedules, METAR/TAF, runway and stand status, ground stops, ATFM caps and NOTAMs, then keeps the operating plan safe as conditions change.

- **Validates** the approved plan continuously and raises conflicts the moment weather or capacity shifts
- **Forecasts** demand against capacity six hours ahead, with a TAF worst-case band for early warnings
- **Re-plans** runway slots and stands together with OR-Tools CP-SAT under hard safety constraints, emergencies first
- **Explains** every change and never applies a recovery plan without human approval

### AgentOffice &nbsp;·&nbsp; An AI office team you can just tell what to do
<sub><b>Open Agent Hackathon 2026</b> · Reasoning Architecture track · <a href="https://github.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/AgentOffice">Repository →</a></sub>

<a href="https://github.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/AgentOffice"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/AgentOffice/main/docs/images/ui-split-at-work.png" width="100%" alt="AgentOffice: the agent team planning, building and testing a spreadsheet automation"/></a>

Attach a spreadsheet and describe the repetitive work. A team of agents plans it, writes the automation, tests it on your real data and asks you to approve the exact code before handing back the filled file, in English or Arabic.

- **13 agents** in capped critique loops, with a QA oracle that is independent of the code it checks
- **Approval bound to a code hash**, a sandbox for every run, and hash-chained traces
- **Live results:** runs that filled 119 and 275 cells, and found ~24 hours a week of automatable finance work
- **231 tests**, plus a dynamic-island UI that floats over every app on Windows and macOS

### GEOS &nbsp;·&nbsp; Early warning for environmental stress
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/%F0%9F%A5%87%201st%20Place-The%20Grand%20Hack%20%C2%B7%20IEI-b8860b?style=flat-square" alt="1st place, The Grand Hack by IEI"/>&nbsp;<sub>Computer Science track · <a href="https://github.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/GEOS">Repository →</a></sub>

<a href="https://github.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/GEOS"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allurimohansrisaivarma-coder/GEOS/main/docs/img/dashboard-full.png" width="100%" alt="GEOS dashboard: per-worker core temperature estimate, forecast, reasons and actions"/></a>

The same 42 °C afternoon endangers a new hire in a double-layer suit and not an acclimatised supervisor in the shade. GEOS works out what is happening *to each person* and warns them before it harms them, even with no network.

- **Real WBGT** per exposure zone from plain weather data and sun position, with CUSUM change-point detection
- **Extended Kalman filter** fusing a heat-strain model with heart rate to estimate each worker's core temperature
- **60-minute forecast** of time-to-danger with uncertainty; every alert says *why* and *what to do*
- **Offline PWA with zero dependencies**: the engine runs on the device, so warnings never wait for a network
